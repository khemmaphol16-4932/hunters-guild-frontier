# Hunter's Guild: Frontier — Art Direction

## Visual north star

A modern 2.5D pixel-art isometric management RPG. The Guild is a warm, active
place worth protecting; wilderness beyond it is beautiful, uncanny, and
dangerous. The player is never shown as an avatar—the Guild, its hunters, and
its accumulated history own the frame.

## MVP asset sequence

1. **Guild Town overview** — the anchor image and palette reference.
2. Guild Hall exterior — the visual identity of the institution.
3. Hunter roster portraits — readable personalities at small UI sizes.
4. Safe-region expedition vista — inviting exploration and visible knowledge.
5. Black-Zone expedition vista — real danger without horror excess.
6. Card and equipment reveal art — high-value collectible moments.

## Asset 01: Guild Town overview

**Use:** title / dashboard background art, concept reference for the Phase 9
isometric town scene.

**Scene:** An elevated three-quarter isometric view of a compact frontier town
at golden hour. A sturdy timber-and-stone Guild Hall anchors the upper-middle
of the scene. A gate and road lead toward the lower edge; a five-hunter party
is returning through it. Include modest housing, a warm lantern-lit service
building, a forge/production corner, food stores, paths, trees, notices, and
working townspeople. The town feels busy but legible, not crowded.

**Mood:** cozy, capable, lived-in, with a faint sense of the untamed frontier
outside the palisade. Warm guild space; cooler forest and distant mountains.

**Style:** premium hand-crafted pixel art with clean readable silhouettes,
soft ambient shading, selective animation-friendly details, and game-ready
layer separation. It is 2.5D/isometric—not a flat tactics map, not a
photorealistic render, not generic medieval realism.

**Composition:** 16:9 landscape. Preserve open, quieter sky / distant-mountain
space along the upper-left for title or dashboard overlays. Use a clear road
and building grouping so the scene can later inform an interactive layout.

**Avoid:** player avatar, modern objects, combat UI, text, logos, watermarks,
excessively tiny detail, a full life-simulation crowd, grim-dark palette.

## Shared visual rules

- Guild palette: honey lantern gold, warm timber brown, faded brick red,
  moss green, slate blue shadows.
- Wilderness palette: cooler desaturated greens/blues; reserve deep crimson,
  violet, and near-black accents for Black-Zone threat.
- Buildings show tier through stronger silhouettes, materials, banners,
  lighting, and grounded additions—not floating level labels.
- Hunters must read as distinct people from stance, kit, and activity before
  their class or stats are known.
- Town history belongs in the world: trophies, plaques, repaired walls,
  monuments, banners, and veteran traces grow over time.

## Asset 04: Verdant Reach vista

**Use:** world-map background and safe-region palette anchor.

The first expedition vista follows the road from the warm guild settlement into
a broad moss-green river valley. Trail markers, camps and a small travelling
party make the guild's accumulated knowledge visible without turning the map
into a tactical combat screen. The generated source is
`generated/verdant-reach-vista-v1.png`.
